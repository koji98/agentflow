# Delivery Review

At terminal state, review in this order:

1. `delivery/manifest.json`
2. `delivery/reviewer-guide.md`
3. `delivery/task-brief.md`
4. `delivery/implementation-summary.md`
5. `delivery/risk-notes.md`
6. `delivery/follow-up-items.md`
7. `delivery/run-map.md`
8. declared artifacts and evidence files named by the manifest
9. raw runtime files only for debug, audit, or resume

The manifest separates human entrypoints, declared artifacts, resume-required files, audit trail, debug-only files, and empty/no-op files.

Review questions:

- Does the reviewer guide explain review order, risks, failed checks, interventions, and follow-ups?
- Do declared artifacts satisfy their descriptions?
- Does validation evidence support the claimed outcome?
- Did supervisor interventions change tactics or reveal missing context?
- Are follow-up items real leftovers rather than skipped acceptance criteria?
