# Aesthetic Directions

Choose one primary direction. Borrow a secondary accent only if the result still reads as one system.

Treat the font and palette examples below as starting points, not defaults. Vary the actual pairings across projects.

## Editorial Precision

Best for:
- content-heavy products
- premium SaaS
- portfolios

Signals:
- high-contrast serif or restrained display face paired with a neutral sans
- paper, ink, and one sharp accent
- strong margins, asymmetry, and controlled pacing
- measured fades and wipes instead of flashy motion

Avoid:
- turning it into generic blog chrome

## Technical Instrument

Best for:
- developer tools
- infra products
- analytics and observability

Signals:
- disciplined mono or grotesk pairings
- darker surfaces with precise accent color use
- dense but ordered information hierarchy
- motion that feels mechanical, responsive, and informative

Avoid:
- overdoing terminal nostalgia until readability suffers

## Warm Luxury Minimal

Best for:
- fintech
- real estate
- premium commerce

Signals:
- elegant serif or high-quality neo-grotesk with generous spacing
- cream, charcoal, bronze, forest, or oxblood accents
- sparse layout with intentional large type moments
- subtle texture, soft lighting, and restrained motion

Avoid:
- sterile whitespace without hierarchy

## Industrial Brutalism

Best for:
- fashion
- events
- culture-forward brands

Signals:
- unapologetic type scale jumps
- hard edges, exposed grids, bold rules, and stark contrast
- composition that feels printed, posted, or assembled
- abrupt but purposeful motion

Avoid:
- random chaos with no structural logic

## Organic Solarpunk

Best for:
- climate
- wellness
- education
- civic products

Signals:
- optimistic warm greens, soil tones, sky tones, and sunlight accents
- organic shapes mixed with engineered layouts
- layered gradients, bloom, paper, or grain textures
- motion that feels buoyant and hopeful

Avoid:
- generic eco cliches like leaf icons everywhere

## Playful Modular

Best for:
- consumer apps
- creative tools
- youth-oriented products

Signals:
- modular cards, confident color blocking, and geometric rhythm
- expressive but highly legible type
- lively iconography and interaction feedback
- short, springy motion with visible affordances

Avoid:
- making every element loud

## Cinematic Data Noir

Best for:
- dashboards
- security
- AI products
- data storytelling

Signals:
- deep backgrounds, controlled glow, and selective high-contrast accents
- sharp hierarchy with spotlight zones
- overlays, frames, and image treatment that feel atmospheric
- directional reveals and crossfades

Avoid:
- unreadable low-contrast dark mode

## Retro-Future Signal

Best for:
- music
- gaming
- experimental consumer brands

Signals:
- type with visible personality
- saturated accents against structured neutrals
- scan lines, grids, poster energy, or subtle distortion
- motion that feels synthesized rather than generic

Avoid:
- copying one nostalgic reference too literally

## Calm Healthcare Clarity

Best for:
- healthcare
- productivity
- workflow-heavy tools

Signals:
- humane, low-friction hierarchy
- soft surfaces with clear contrast and spacing
- reassuring accent use and gentle depth
- micro-motion that reinforces trust and progress

Avoid:
- reducing everything to bland pastel cards

## Dense Expert Console

Best for:
- admin tools
- back-office interfaces
- research products

Signals:
- compact but readable spacing system
- tabular numerals and high information density
- obvious section framing and keyboard-first states
- feedback tuned for fast expert workflows

Avoid:
- visual clutter without grouping or hierarchy

## Selection Heuristics

- If the product has emotional storytelling or brand narrative, bias toward editorial, luxury, or cinematic directions.
- If the product is workflow-heavy, bias toward technical instrument, calm clarity, or dense expert console.
- If the product needs memorability more than neutrality, bias toward brutalism, retro-future, or playful modular.
- If the current codebase already leans strongly in one direction, sharpen it instead of changing families entirely.
