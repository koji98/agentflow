# Delivery Checklist

Use this before finalizing frontend work.

## Concept

- one clear aesthetic direction is visible within a few seconds
- the interface has a memorable move or point of view
- typography, color, spacing, and motion all support the same concept

## Structure

- hierarchy is obvious without relying on color alone
- desktop and mobile layouts both feel designed, not merely collapsed
- cards, sections, and navigation follow a consistent spacing rhythm

## Interaction

- hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, empty, and error states exist where needed
- keyboard navigation remains intact
- touch targets are not tiny or crowded on mobile

## Accessibility and Readability

- contrast is readable in all themes
- line lengths, font sizes, and dense data areas remain scannable
- focus treatment is visible and deliberate

## Polish

- there are no stray default styles, placeholder copy, or off-theme states
- shadows, radii, borders, and icon treatment feel like one system
- motion is purposeful and can be disabled without breaking the experience

## Anti-Slop Check

- the design is not interchangeable with a generic AI SaaS landing page
- palette, type pairing, and composition are specific to this product context
- the surface would still look intentional if screenshots were placed beside strong human-made references
